01 / THE IDEA

A calm interface for a world of sound.

Healing Frequencies brings together a broad catalog of frequency traditions in a simple listening environment. Choose a tone, watch the harmonic visualization, adjust the sound and make it part of your own ritual.

Designed for mindful listening and exploration. The app does not claim to diagnose, prevent or treat medical conditions.
