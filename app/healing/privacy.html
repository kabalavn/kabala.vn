LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED · SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Healing Frequencies by Kabastro (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application and this website.

In short: Healing Frequencies is designed to work primarily on your device. The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

1. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the App. In particular, the App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Some app preferences, such as your favorite frequencies and settings, may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its features.

2. Audio and Device Features

The App generates audio tones locally on your Android device. Audio generated by the App is not uploaded to Kabastro as part of the normal operation of the frequency generator.

The App may use device capabilities required for its functionality, such as audio playback and vibration. These capabilities are used to provide the experience you request.

3. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The current version of the App does not intentionally use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking technologies.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when the relevant functionality is introduced.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may provide links to external services, such as Google Play or websites containing educational information. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

5. Data Security

Because the App is designed to minimize personal data collection, there is no central personal-data account associated with normal use of the App. Local information stored by the App is subject to the security controls of your Android device.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, no such information is intentionally requested through the App.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Healing Frequencies is a sound and frequency exploration application. Information and frequency descriptions provided by the App are for educational and informational purposes only. The App is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact the Kabastro team through the official Kabastro website.
